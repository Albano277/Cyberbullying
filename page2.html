What Steps Can We Take When Cyberbullying Happens?

When cyberbullying happens, adults can help by taking the situation seriously, supporting the child, understanding what happened, and taking appropriate action. Because cyberbullying happens digitally, evidence can also be saved and reported.

Actions to Take

  1. Talk to a trusted adult, such as a teacher, counselor, parent, or another trusted person.
  2. Save messages, pictures, or other information as evidence, and report the situation to the school so the appropriate adults can be contacted.
  3. Report cyberbullying when you see someone bullying another person online.
  4. Support the child and consider taking a break from online devices until the situation has been addressed.
  5. Speak up right away and block the individual who is engaging in cyberbullying.

Ways Parents Can Help Prevent Cyberbullying

In addition to responding when cyberbullying happens, parents and caregivers can take steps to help prevent it. They can talk openly with children about online behavior and safety, review privacy and security settings, establish rules for appropriate online behavior, stay aware of the apps and platforms children use, and make sure children know how to report cyberbullying.

If Cyberbullying Happens Online

If cyberbullying occurs online, do not respond to or forward the messages. Instead, keep evidence of what happened and block the person responsible. Cyberbullying can also be reported to the online platform where it occurred.
